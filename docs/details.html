Earnings growth, year over year, season to date

Blended growth, FactSet's measure: actual EPS for companies that have reported by that day, consensus for the rest, times shares outstanding, against the same companies' year-ago EPS. Each line starts at the growth analysts expected before the first report (dashed, kept for reference) and moves as actuals replace estimates: up when companies beat, down when they miss. Estimate revisions during the quarter are not replayed, so this is the results-driven part of the move. Hover for the growth among reporters alone and the day's biggest mover. Dotted: the same growth ex-Alphabet & Amazon (gains on their Anthropic stakes dominate their reported EPS; FactSet footnotes the same exclusion).

Year-ago EPS for earlier seasons is the actual on the year-ago calendar row and is not split-adjusted; the current season uses Nasdaq's split-adjusted year-ago figure where it was captured before the report.
