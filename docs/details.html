Earnings vs consensus, season to date

Earnings dollars above consensus so far (actual minus consensus EPS, times shares outstanding, summed over reporters), as a share of the season's total expected earnings (consensus EPS times shares for every company with a calendar row). 0 is in line with consensus: the line rises as companies beat and falls as they miss, and ends the season at the aggregate EPS surprise for the whole index. Consensus is the Zacks figure on report day, so an estimate raised earlier in the quarter is part of the baseline, not the beat. Hover for the running aggregate surprise among reporters and the day's biggest contributor.

Left out of this chart, in numerator and denominator: companies whose EPS surprise is beyond plus or minus 50% of consensus or whose consensus is zero. A gap that size is almost always a GAAP actual against an adjusted consensus, or a near-zero estimate, rather than a beat, and one such row can move this index-level line by several points.
